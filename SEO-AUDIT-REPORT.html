01

Executive summary

The audit's recommended first move was to fix location identity and contact clarity, then publish the four core service pages, verify technical indexing and establish measurable enquiry tracking. The website-controlled part of that sequence is now in place.

What changed in this release: four dedicated service landing pages, three clinician-review-flagged condition guides, an Our Team page, a working parent-guides section with two articles, clearer local homepage messaging, prominent call / WhatsApp / booking paths on mobile, enquiry click tracking, and consistent structured data. The sitemap, canonicals, robots file and page metadata were checked and rebuilt.
What did not change: anything that requires Google Business Profile manager access, Search Console verification, clinic decisions about branches and services, therapist-approved credentials and medical wording, real clinic photographs with consent, or corrections on external directories. These are listed in section 04 — none have been guessed at or quietly removed.

What was done

Each item below maps to a number in the original issues register or the 30-day checklist.

FixedRegister #03

Service-page architecture

Four separate, indexable landing pages now exist for the core therapies instead of one combined block.

  • Speech therapy, occupational therapy, behaviour therapy, special education
  • Each has symptoms / suitability, how sessions work, FAQs and a booking CTA
  • Every page carries Service + FAQPage + breadcrumb schema
FixedRegister #09

Condition / support guides

Three parent-facing guides were added for the discovery gaps in the audit.

  • Autism support, ADHD-related support, speech delay
  • No diagnosis, cure or outcome promises
  • Flagged for therapist sign-off before being treated as final
FixedRegister #06, #20

Blog navigation & content

The broken / missing Blog destination is now a real, functioning article index.

  • /blog/ with two published parent articles
  • Author / reviewer framing, dates, reading time, clinical disclaimer
  • Blog added to the main navigation and footer
FixedRegister #07

Homepage positioning

The generic hero was rewritten around the actual service and locality intent.

  • New title: Child Development & Therapies in L.B. Nagar
  • New H1 and opening copy naming the four therapies and L.B. Nagar
  • Unique meta description, page schema and Open Graph tags inherited
FixedRegister #05, #08, #14

Contact clarity & parent intent

Enquiry routes are now visible and the information parents need is on the relevant page.

  • Tap-to-call in the header; sticky mobile bar with Call / WhatsApp / Book
  • First-visit, assessment, session-format and "contact for current rates" wording added
  • FAQs answer suitability, duration, online options and next steps
FixedRegister #16, #18

Structured data & internal links

One source of truth now drives the business details in schema.

  • Address derived from site.ts rather than duplicated
  • LocalBusiness / MedicalBusiness, Service, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList, Article
  • Homepage, services page, footer and every new page cross-linked
FixedRegister #19

Enquiry tracking

Rankings alone cannot show which clicks turn into enquiries, so events were added.

  • GA4 enquiry_click events for call, WhatsApp and booking clicks
  • Events carry only the click type — no personal data or child details
  • Header, CTA bands, new pages and mobile bar are all tagged
FixedRegister #17, #15, #01

Indexing, trust & NAP hygiene

Technical foundations were re-generated and checked.

  • 18 clean URLs in sitemap-index.xml; 404 excluded; robots file points to it
  • Testimonial disclaimer added: stories shared with permission, progress varies
  • Vanasthalipuram removed from schema areaServed to reduce conflicting signals; no business listing was deleted
PartialRegister #04

Our Team page

A dedicated team page now exists, but it could not be completed honestly.

  • Shows the four disciplines and how the team coordinates care

  • Still needed: named therapists, qualifications, registrations, portraits with consent

08

Important notes

No ranking or lead promises. Local rankings depend on relevance, distance and prominence, and public review counts change. Targets here are activity targets, not forecasts.
Clinical review is mandatory. The autism, ADHD, speech-delay and blog content was written for parents and kept deliberately conservative, but a qualified therapist must approve it before it is treated as final. Testimonials need permission and should never identify a child or describe treatment details.
Do not delete listings. The Vanasthalipuram question is marked "verify", not "remove". Confirm ownership and operating status before any merge, correction or deletion.
